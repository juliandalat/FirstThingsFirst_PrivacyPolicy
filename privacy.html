Privacy Policy

First Things First · Last updated August 20th, 2026

First Things First does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, and no analytics or tracking software of any kind. We do not have the ability to see your data, because it never reaches us.

What stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your iPhone and is never uploaded anywhere:

  • Your routines and the steps in them
  • Your schedule and wake time
  • Your completion history, streaks, and rest days
  • Your app settings

If you delete the app, this data is removed with it. We keep no copy, so it cannot be recovered.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple ID. The app checks your subscription status through Apple's system on your device.

Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregate sales and usage reports — for example, the total number of downloads in a country. These reports contain no information that identifies you.

Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server, and no notification content is sent over the internet.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries. We do not sell, share, or transfer data to anyone, because we hold none.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. Your data is under your direct control on your own device.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes what it collects, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact
